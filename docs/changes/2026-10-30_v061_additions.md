# Spec v0.4 to v0.6.1 additions

The v0.6.1 spec and its screen designs are added to the platform as new modules. Nothing in the existing nine-stage audit was replaced. This file records what was built, the decisions taken where the spec was silent or unclear, and what is still to do. It is updated as the work continues, in the same way as the 27 October change log.

Status key: **Done**, **Partly done**, **Not started**.

## What is new, by area

Seeded requirements, each with citation, source link and a quoted passage, cover NYC LL144, Colorado SB 26-189, Illinois HB 3773, the EU AI Act (including the 2026 amendments that move high-risk duties to 2 December 2027), GDPR, India's DPDP Act, the California CCPA automated decision-making rules and the California Civil Rights Council rules on automated-decision systems, the CERT-In Directions and NIST AI RMF.

To check: the Colorado section numbers come from the engrossed bill and are flagged until checked against the enrolled act. Smaller items are also flagged in the records: GDPR Art. 83(5) and Art. 6 mapping, the EU AI Act Art. 17 basis, two CCPA section numbers, the DPDP rule number and the Illinois rules status.

## Fixes made while integrating

- The first request after signing in was sometimes refused. Every request now saves its changes before it answers.
- A slow address lookup in new client set-up could lock the database ("database is locked"). Fixed.
- The live law refresh would have failed on its first run, because Claude's API does not return structured data and cite web sources in the same request. The refresh now searches first and structures the findings in a second step.
- The client page's contacts tab shows the client's email connection, with a link to its email groups.

## Fixes from the independent review

Three separate reviews (security, data, screens) looked at all the new code. Every finding graded critical or important, and the minor ones worth fixing, was fixed with a test written first that failed before the fix.

### Law library and refresh

- Editing a law in Reference data creates a new row for it. Client statuses, approved changes, the intro letter and the "changes since" banner now follow the law to its current row instead of staying on the old one. Old links on the laptop are moved on first start.
- Adding back a law that was removed from a client no longer fails.
- A change proposed against an older version of a law cannot be approved over a newer one.
- New laws and new court cases found by the refresh are matched against existing records by jurisdiction and name or citation, and by court and docket number, so the same law or case is not added twice.
- A proposed change whose link or quote was not actually cited by Claude's web search is marked low confidence. Changes based only on a secondary source about a court case are also marked low confidence.
- An edit made after the second reviewer agreed goes back to that reviewer before it is applied.
- A repeal with a future date keeps the law in scope until that date.
- "Not yet in force" and "pending" now show as pending.
- The refresh schedule is off until the Chief Auditor turns it on.
- The client's AI system register now feeds the applicability check, and changes to a system re-run it.

### Email, alerts and deadlines

- Two background jobs could send the same email or incident reminder twice. Each send is now claimed once.
- Date alerts are no longer sent again when a new version of the law is approved.
- The client's "AI Governance Committee" email group was always empty. It now lists the committee members.

### Register and add-ons

- Go-live is refused while the role catalogue has not loaded, and Futran Admins are told once if loading failed.
- Approving, declining or cancelling the tracing add-on now shows on the Sales opportunity.
- Database updates run one at a time, and each one either completes or leaves the database as it was.

### Screens

- GR-02 in the governance request (10a) is the bias and fairness review committee again, with its committee fields.
- An auditor can link a fieldwork system to the client's register from the Edit dialog.
- The Legal Reviewer and QA Reviewer reach the refresh review queue from the main menu. Roles that cannot use the queue no longer see it.
- Buttons that a role cannot use are hidden (the client applicability button for QA and the Engagement Manager).
- On a phone the header collapses into a menu, so the page starts near the top of the screen.
- After signing in, a person lands on the page they were trying to open, and links to other sites are refused.
- Buttons can no longer be pressed twice to send the same request twice.
- Field help added on the new inputs; touch targets on phones are at least 44 px.

## Decisions for you

1. **Go-live needs two people.** One person submits and a different Chief AI Compliance Officer approves. A company with only one CAICO and no department heads cannot take a system live.
2. **Tracing turns on at sponsor approval.** The spec says after Sales confirms the order. A "Sales confirms" step can be added.
3. **Futran Admin can manage client email groups** after onboarding too; the §26.9 table says no.
4. **Engagement Manager and Legal Reviewer land on My work**, as §13.1 gives them no home of their own.

## What IT needs to set up for real email

Not needed for test mode.

- **Futran IT, once:** register the platform in Microsoft Entra ID and in Google Cloud, then put the app IDs and secrets in the platform's settings file. Step-by-step instructions are in the README.
- **Client IT, once per client:** a Microsoft 365 administrator approves the app from the Connect button. A Google Workspace administrator marks the app as trusted, which avoids Google's security review for outside apps.

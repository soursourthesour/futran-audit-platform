ABC Corp AI Acceptable Use Policy

1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets the rules for how ABC Corp staff, contractors and temporary workers use artificial intelligence tools in their work, in all locations and departments.

2. Approved tools

Staff may use only: Microsoft Copilot (company account) and the ATS screening system. Use of personal or free AI accounts for ABC Corp work is not allowed.

3. Data you must never enter

Client or customer data; applicant and employee personal data; source code and credentials.

4. Decisions about people

No AI output may be the sole basis for a hiring, promotion, discipline or termination decision. A trained person reviews and can override every AI recommendation …

On approval: the policy is published, a staff acknowledgment campaign starts, and the commitment closes with this policy as evidence. Want Futran to tailor it? Advisory customization can be quoted; the Futran team that tailors it cannot audit it later.

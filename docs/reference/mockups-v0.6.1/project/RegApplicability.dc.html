4. Rules for each type of AI you use

AI agents (2)
  • Name a stop authority and a permissions approver
  • Approve the tools and data each agent may use
  • Test before release; log every action
AI or ML software deciding about people (2)
  • Bias audit within the past 12 months (NYC)
  • Notice to candidates and employees (NYC, Illinois)
  • Consent before AI video analysis (Illinois)
Embedded AI features (2)
  • Record the vendor and data terms
  • Train users (EU AI Act Art. 4)
  • No confidential data unless contract allows
Generative AI tools and chatbots
  • Tell users they are talking to AI (EU AI Act Art. 50)
  • Approved tools list; no personal tools for company data

Futran reviews these laws every two weeks. When a change affects you, you get an alert on the platform and an updated letter. Rule versions used in this letter: EU AI Act v5, NYC LL144 v2, Illinois HB 3773 v3, Illinois AI Video Interview Act v1.

Committee and program rules

AI Governance CommitteeRecommended: NIST GOVERN 2.1 and 3.1, ISO/IEC 42001 §5.3, India AI Governance Guidelines
  • Chair: accountable executive
  • Members: AI Compliance Officer, Legal, DPO, technical lead, a business or HR lead
  • Approves Tier 3 systems and exceptions
  • Meets at least quarterly
Consequential-decision rightsRequired: Colorado SB 26-189 (from 1 Jan 2027), CCPA ADMT (from 1 Jan 2027)
  • Notice before use
  • Explanation after an adverse outcome; data correction
  • Human review or appeal
  • Colorado no longer requires a risk program or impact assessments
Quality management systemRequired: EU AI Act Art. 17 (providers of high-risk AI)
  • Accountability framework with named roles
  • Post-market monitoring and incident reporting
  • Applies only if the client builds and sells high-risk AI
Significant data fiduciary set-upRequired: India DPDP Act §10 (if designated)
  • DPO based in India
  • Independent data auditor
  • Periodic data protection impact assessment

Scope tests used at client set-up

The client applicability check asks these. A "yes" on 1 and 2 puts the client in scope.

1Does the client make decisions about Colorado residents?
2Does any AI system materially influence a consequential decision: employment, housing, credit, insurance, health care, education or government services?
3Is the client the developer, the deployer, or both? (Developer duties: documentation and 3-year retention. Deployer duties: notice, explanation, correction, human review.)

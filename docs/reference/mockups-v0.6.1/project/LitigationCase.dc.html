What it is about

A job applicant says AI screening tools used by many employers rejected him because of his age, race and disability. The court let the claims go ahead against the software vendor itself, as the employers' agent, not only against the employers.

Why it matters to our clients

  • Employers stay liable for a vendor's screening tool; the vendor can be liable too.
  • Age effects (applicants 40+) need testing, not only sex and race.
  • Courts may order vendors to name the employers using a feature.

Talking points

For the Chief Auditor and Sales Team
  • "If you use a vendor's screening tool, you share the risk. When was its last independent bias audit?"
  • Offer: LL144-style bias audit including age; vendor due-diligence review.
  • Offer: tracing module for hiring tools, to show who decided what.
For the client's Chief AI Compliance Officer (sent in the briefing)
  • List every hiring tool that ranks or filters applicants.
  • Ask each vendor for its bias audit, including age.
  • Keep a person reviewing rejections, and keep records for 4 years.
Not legal advice. Clients should confirm with counsel.
